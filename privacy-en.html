Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · 한국어

Bubbly has no sign-up and no servers. Your tasks and settings are stored only on this device and are never sent to anyone, including the people who make Bubbly. There are no ads and no analytics.

1. What is stored, and where

  • Tasks, dates and times, importance, faces, repeats, settings and bubbles received from friends are stored only in the app's storage on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.
  • On iPhone, a copy of your data is also kept in your own iCloud account (key-value storage) so it can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling. Apple manages that storage inside your account; Bubbly never reads it.
  • If you use your phone's device backup (Google account backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), the operating system may include the app's data in that backup. Those backups are managed by the operating system and your account.

2. Device permissions

  • Writing to your calendar (optional): only while “Write bubbles to phone calendar” is on, Bubbly creates one “Bubbly” calendar and writes your timed bubbles there as events. Other calendars are never changed; turning it off deletes those events.
  • Reminders (optional · iPhone): only while “Import Reminders” is on, Bubbly reads your open reminders as bubbles and marks a reminder done when you pop its bubble. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Calendar (optional): only when you turn on "Bring in phone calendar" in Settings, Bubbly reads event titles and times to show them as bubbles. It only reads and never changes your calendar.
  • Notifications (optional): only when you turn them on in Settings, Bubbly schedules a reminder 30 minutes before timed tasks, on your device.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap "Receive" in Settings; written only when you copy text while sending.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, such as when popping a bubble. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Location (optional): only while “Match the weather” is on in Settings, your approximate location (two decimals, about 1 km) is sent to the weather service Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current weather code, which tints the sea and adds rain or snow. Once an hour; the location is not stored and goes nowhere else. Turning it off stops it immediately.

3. Sharing bubbles with friends

  • When you tap "Send", a message is created containing the task and its timing, plus the mood, face, note and name you chose. It is sent through an app you pick (a messenger, SMS, etc.). Bubbly does not keep this message or send it to any server.
  • Received bubbles are kept on your device for up to 30 days until you accept them, then removed.
  • "I finished it" replies are sent only when you send them yourself.

4. Problem log

When an error happens, Bubbly keeps up to the 20 most recent error messages, their location in the code, and the app version on your device. Task contents are never included. It leaves your device only if you tap "Copy problem log" in Settings and send it yourself.

5. Backup files

Backup files you create with "Export data" are saved where you choose. Bubbly does not send them anywhere.

6. Children

Bubbly collects no personal information, including from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and mention it in the app's update notes.

8. Contact

Please use the developer contact listed on the app store page.
