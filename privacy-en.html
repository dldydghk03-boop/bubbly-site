Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Free version 1.0

You can use Bubbly without an account. Your tasks and daily notes are stored on your device. On iPhone, iCloud can sync records across devices using the same Apple Account. When you share a bubble, its contents go to the app you choose. Bubbly uses no analytics tools.

1. What is stored, and where

  • Tasks, dates and times, importance, faces, repeats, settings and bubbles received from friends are stored in the app's storage on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data from your device. However, the iCloud copy may remain.
  • On iPhone, a copy of your data is also kept in your own iCloud account (key-value storage) so it can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling, and kept in step with your other iPhones on the same account. It includes settings, tasks, the received bubble inbox, AI diaries and saved versions, daily notes with edit/deletion history, and deletion records. Sample tasks and pending items imported from the phone calendar or Reminders are excluded; finished or past imported items may be included. Large backups may exclude some older completed tasks from iCloud. Diary versions are never trimmed to fit. If iCloud cannot hold everything, the app advises exporting a complete backup file. Apple manages that storage inside your account; Bubbly's developers do not access it.
  • If you use your phone's device backup (Google account backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), the operating system may include the app's data in that backup. Those backups are managed by the operating system and your account.

2. Device permissions

No permission is required to use Bubbly. Everything below is optional: you can use the rest of the app without allowing it, and you can change it anytime in your phone's settings.

  • Writing to your calendar (optional): only while “Write bubbles to phone calendar” is on, Bubbly creates one “Bubbly” calendar and writes your timed bubbles there as events. Other calendars are never changed; turning it off deletes those events.
  • Reminders (optional · iPhone): only while “Import Reminders” is on, Bubbly reads your open reminders as bubbles and updates a reminder when you finish or undo its bubble. Finished records may be included in iCloud storage (section 1).
  • Calendar (optional): only when you turn on "Bring in phone calendar" in Settings, Bubbly reads event titles and times to show them as bubbles. It only reads and never changes your calendar.
  • Notifications (optional): only when you turn them on in Settings, Bubbly schedules a reminder 30 minutes before timed tasks, on your device.
  • Camera · photos (optional): used only when you take or pick a photo for your face bubble. The photo is shrunk and kept on this phone. When you choose to send it, it is included in the code and link, and in the KakaoTalk card image (section 3). Records containing photos may also be included in device backups and iCloud storage (section 1).
  • Clipboard: read when you tap "Receive" in Settings. Bubbly also checks for a friend bubble code each time the app starts on Android, and once on the first launch after installation on iPhone. The check is deferred during the initial guide. You are asked whether to receive something only when a receivable bubble or reply code is found; the text read is not sent outside the device. The clipboard is written only when you copy text while sending.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, such as when popping a bubble. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Motion sensors (optional): used while the annual sea is open, with permission requested when required by your device. Motion values are processed on this device, never stored or transmitted. Reading stops when you close the view or leave the app.

3. Sharing bubbles with friends

  • When you tap "Send", a code and link are created containing the task and its timing, importance and repeat details, plus the mood, face, note and name you chose. They are sent through an app you pick (a messenger, SMS, etc.). If you choose a "My face" photo, it is also included in the code. Anyone with the link can read its contents. Bubbly does not keep this message on a separate sharing server.
  • Sending via KakaoTalk: when you tap "Send via KakaoTalk" in the app, a picture of the bubble is uploaded to Kakao's server for the card (if you chose "My face" when sending, your face photo is in that picture), and the card's title (your name), description (the task, its timing and your note) and the bubble inside its button are passed to KakaoTalk. Kakao's privacy policy applies to this part. To avoid KakaoTalk, choose "Send with another app".
  • Received bubbles are kept on your device for up to 30 days until you accept them, then removed.
  • "I finished it" replies are sent only when you send them yourself.

4. AI diary and daily notes

In version 1.0, you can write and save a note for each day. This version does not generate AI diary entries or send your tasks or notes to an AI service.

5. Problem log

When an error happens, Bubbly keeps up to the 20 most recent error messages, their location in the code, and the app version on your device. Task contents are never included. It leaves your device only if you tap "Copy problem log" in Settings and send it yourself.

6. Backup files

Backup files you create with "Export data" are saved where you choose. Bubbly does not send them anywhere.

7. Purchases and ads

This version is free and includes no ads or in-app purchases. Bubbly does not send user information to advertising or payment services.

8. Children

Bubbly does not ask your age. The information processing described above also applies when children use these features.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and mention it in the app's update notes.
